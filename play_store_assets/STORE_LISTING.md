```text
Take complete control of your financial life with SpendWise — the modern, private, and effortless expense tracker and budget planner designed to help you save more and spend smarter.

Unlike most finance apps that sell your data, require sign-ups, or spam you with loans and ads, SpendWise is 100% offline-first. Your financial data stays stored safely on your device — strictly private to you.

🌟 WHY SPENDWISE?
• 100% Offline & Private: No account creation or cloud servers. Your finances are your business alone.
• Zero Ads: A peaceful, distraction-free money management experience.
• Lightning Fast: Add income and expenses in just two taps.
• Clean & Beautiful: Sleek modern dark mode and intuitive charts.

🔥 KEY FEATURES

📊 Intuitive Expense & Income Tracking
• Log daily transactions in seconds with rich categories, notes, and dates.
• Filter and search transaction history effortlessly.
• Instant breakdown of daily, weekly, and monthly cash flow.

🎯 Smart Monthly Budgets
• Set spending limits for individual categories (Food, Shopping, Bills, Transport, etc.).
• Real-time visual progress bars warn you before you overspend.
• Stay disciplined and hit your monthly savings targets.

💰 Savings Goals & Tracking
• Create dedicated savings targets for emergencies, travel, gadgets, or education.
• Track your progress with animated milestone bars and celebration prompts.

🏷️ Custom Categories & Colors
• Personalize your tracking with customizable categories, vibrant colors, and modern icons.
• Organize your money your way.

🤝 Debt & Loan (IOU) Manager
• Keep clear track of money you borrowed or lent to friends and family.
• Easily link contacts and mark balances as settled with a single tap.

📱 WhatsApp & PDF Financial Reports
• Generate clean financial summaries formatted for quick sharing via WhatsApp, email, or messaging apps.
• Export your full transaction history to CSV spreadsheet anytime.

🔒 Biometric Security (Fingerprint & PIN)
• Protect your financial privacy from prying eyes with Android BiometricPrompt fingerprint and PIN lock.

🌍 Multi-Currency Support
```
